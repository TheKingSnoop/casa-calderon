## Ingredients

### Meatballs

- Minced meat: 60% pork, 40% beef
- Dry seasoning:
  - Salt
  - White pepper
  - Garlic
  - Onion (or finely diced fresh onion, if you have time)
  - Chinese five spice
  - Breadcrumbs
- Coriander stems, finely sliced

### Sauce

- Gochujang
- Rice vinegar
- Soy sauce
- Honey
- Ketchup

## Method

1. Mix the meat with the dry seasoning and coriander stems, then roll into 35–40 g balls.
2. Fry the meatballs until browned all over.
3. Transfer them to the oven at 350°F (175°C) for about 10 minutes, depending on how long you fried them.
4. Make the sauce in the same pan, using the fond (the browned bits left from frying).
5. Bring the meatballs back to the pan and coat them well in the sauce.

## To serve

- With noodles, pasta or rice
- Steamed broccoli or other vegetables
